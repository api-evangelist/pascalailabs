# Pascal AI (Pascal AI Labs)

> Pascal AI is an institutional-finance AI company building context-driven, MCP-native research agents that turn enterprise and market data into investment insights for hedge funds, asset managers, investment banks, and private-markets firms. The platform runs inside Excel, PowerPoint, Word, email, and the browser, ships every output with a citation trail to the source line, and deploys inside the customer VPC or on-premises. Founded 2024 (Bangalore); backed by Kalaari Capital, Norwest Venture Partners, Antler, and Info Edge Ventures ($3.1M seed, 2025). No public API specification or developer portal is published; the API & SDK (Python, TypeScript, REST) and MCP connectors are enterprise/gated.
